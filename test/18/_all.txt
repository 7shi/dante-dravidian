# Italian

52 questa mi porse tanto di gravezza
53 con la paura ch'uscia di sua vista,
54 ch'io perdei la speranza de l'altezza.

# English

—she caused me so much heaviness, with the fear that came from sight of her, that I lost hope of the height.

# Telugu

54 నేను ఎత్తు పట్ల ఆశను కోల్పోయాను.

# Tamil

52 அவள் எனக்கு இவ்வளவு கனத்த மனச்சுமையை அளித்தாள்

54 நான் உயரத்தின் நம்பிக்கையை இழந்தேன்.

# Kannada

# Malayalam

53 അവളുടെ ദർശനത്തിൽ നിന്നു ഉദിച്ച ഭയത്തോടുകൂടി,
