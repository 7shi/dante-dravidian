# Italian

67 Rispuosemi: «Non omo, omo già fui,
68 e li parenti miei furon lombardi,
69 mantoani per patrïa ambedui.

# English

He answered me: “Not man; man once I was, and my parents were Lombards, and Mantuans by country both.
